Help

FAQ and troubleshooting

The questions that come up most often, answered with what is actually happening rather than "clear your cache". If yours is not here, write to us.

Content and demo data

My grids show clothes I never added

Featured looks and Product grid both have a source setting. It ships as demo, which renders bundled sample pieces. Open the section in the editor and switch the source to a collection or a hand-picked product list. The demo pieces then disappear entirely — they are not products and cannot be purchased.

Can I delete the bundled photographs?

You can, but sections that fall back to them will then render an empty frame. The cleaner route is to upload your own images in each section's image picker; a picked image always wins over the bundled one. Every bundled asset is a normal file in assets/ if you do want to remove them.

A section I added is not in the "Add section" list on some pages

Home-page sections only appear when the home page is being edited, and product or collection sections only appear on those templates. This is how Shopify scopes sections; it is not a missing section.

Storefront behaviour

The collection filters are missing

show_filters on the collection template uses Shopify's own storefront filtering, which needs the free Search & Discovery app installed and at least one filter configured for that collection. Without it the filter rail has nothing to render and stays hidden.

The quick filters on the product grid do not find everything

Those filter the cards already rendered on the page — they are a convenience, not a search. Filtering your whole catalogue happens on collection pages. Set limit high enough that the pieces you want to be filterable are actually on the page.

There is no country or language selector in the footer

The selectors only render when there is a choice to make. Add a second country under Settings → Markets, or a second language under Settings → Languages. A single-market store shows neither, deliberately — a dropdown with one option is noise.

Saved looks vanished

Saving is stored in the browser, on the customer's device, under the key pawform.state.v1. It survives closing the tab and restarting the browser, and it is lost when browsing data is cleared, in private windows, and when the customer switches device or browser. Nothing is stored on your store, so there is nothing for you to recover — and equally, nothing that needs a privacy notice beyond the cookie policy you already have.

Adding to bag reloads the page instead of opening the drawer

Check enable_cart_drawer in Cart and checkout. If it is on, the cause is usually JavaScript being blocked or failing to load; the product form then falls back to a normal post to /cart, which is the intended safety net.

The announcement bar scrolls too fast, or not at all

Speed lives in two places: marquee_speed globally, and speed on the Marquee section for the large one on the home page. The announcement bar follows the global value. Both respect the operating system's reduced-motion setting, in which case the text stops moving.

Fonts and styling

The fonts do not match the screenshots

The theme ships with Shopify font pickers enabled (Assistant and Playfair Display). The screenshots use the bundled typefaces. Turn on use_bundled_fonts in Typography to get the look from the preview. See Typography and fonts for the trade-offs.

All the text got smaller after I changed the type scale

The type scale multiplies every size in the theme, so 90% shrinks headings along with body copy. If only body text feels too large, change the body font in the picker rather than the scale — the scale is the blunt instrument for "the whole theme is too small for my audience".

Text is overflowing a button or a card

Almost always caused by long unbroken words — a URL in a heading, a long product name in a narrow column. Add a space or shorten the label. The theme wraps at word boundaries rather than breaking words mid-character, which is the right behaviour for reading and the wrong one for exceptional strings.

Do I have to keep PF-FONT-LICENSES.txt?

Yes. The four bundled typefaces are under the SIL Open Font License, which requires the licence to be distributed with them. Deleting the file is a licence breach even though nothing visibly breaks.

Customer accounts

Logging in sends customers to shopify.com

Your store is using Shopify's new customer accounts, where the login and account area are hosted by Shopify. The theme's account templates are not used in that mode, and their styling does not apply. See Customer accounts.

The account pages look unstyled

They use their own stylesheet, assets/pawform-account.css, which loads only on account templates. If it is missing from your theme's file list, re-upload the theme — a single dropped asset during upload produces exactly this symptom.

An order page returns "not found"

Order pages are per-customer. A link copied from one account will not open in another, and signed-out visitors are redirected to the login page. That is expected.

Compatibility

Does it work with Shopify Markets?

Yes. Prices, currency and the country selector all follow Markets. Products need a price adjustment for the market or Shopify falls back to the store's base price.

Can I translate it?

Two locales ship — English and Simplified Chinese — and all interface strings (buttons, form labels, account pages, error messages) live in the locale files rather than in templates. Section copy such as headings and descriptions is per-section text you set in the editor, so it is translated by editing it, not by adding a locale key.

Does it support metafields?

Only through the Custom Liquid block, or through app blocks. The theme does not have metafield-driven sections, so product specifications belong in the product description or the Details block.

Will my apps' blocks show up?

App blocks appear wherever the section schema allows them: the product template, the collection template and the look grids. If an app's block does not appear, the app has not declared one for that template type.
